The Chartered Accountancy (CA) course, governed by The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), is a globally recognized professional program designed to create experts in financial reporting, auditing, taxation, and business advisory, typically spanning 4 to 4.5 years. The educational journey begins with CA Foundation, an entry-level exam for Class 12 graduates covering Accounting, Business Laws, Quantitative Aptitude, and Business Economics over a 4-month preparation period. Next is CA Intermediate, spanning 8 months of study and divided into Group I (Advanced Accounting, Corporate and Other Laws, and Taxation) and Group II (Cost and Management Accounting, Auditing and Ethics, and Financial Management and Strategic Management). Following intermediate exams, students undergo 2 years of mandatory practical training (articleship) under a practicing Chartered Accountant for hands-on professional experience. Finally, the CA Final level tests advanced strategic and multidisciplinary decision-making across Group I (Financial Reporting, Advanced Financial Management, Advanced Auditing, Assurance and Professional Ethics) and Group II (Direct Tax Laws and International Taxation, Indirect Tax Laws, and Integrated Business Solutions).
